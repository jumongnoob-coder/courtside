START WITH THE BASICS

BASKETBALL FUNDAMENTALS

🏀 Dribbling

Dribbling is bouncing the ball continuously with one hand while moving around the court.

🎯 Shooting

Shooting is the skill of throwing the ball toward the basket to score points.

🤝 Passing

Passing is giving the ball to a teammate to create opportunities and maintain teamwork.

🛡️ Defense

Defense is preventing the opposing team from scoring by guarding players and protecting the basket.

UNDERSTAND THE GAME

BASIC BASKETBALL RULES

01. Team Players

Each team has five players on the court during regular play.

02. Traveling

Players cannot take illegal steps while holding the ball.

03. Double Dribble

A player cannot start dribbling again after ending a dribble.

04. Fouls

Illegal physical contact may result in a personal foul.

05. Scoring

A free throw is worth one point, a regular field goal two, and a shot beyond the three-point line three.

IMPROVE YOUR GAME

TRAINING TIPS

🏃 Warm Up

Prepare your body with light movement and stretching before playing.

⛹️ Practice Daily

Practice basic skills regularly and focus on proper technique.

💧 Stay Hydrated

Drink enough water before, during, and after physical activity.
