HISTORY OF BASKETBALL

How Basketball Started

Basketball was invented in 1891 by Dr. James Naismith in Springfield, Massachusetts, USA. He created the game to give students an indoor sport during the winter.

Over time, basketball became one of the most popular sports in the world. Today, millions of people enjoy playing and watching basketball.
